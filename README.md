## Overview

Insurance claims are often the most stressful customer interaction. While Northbridge Insurance provides an online claims pathway, there is an opportunity to improve transparency, guidance, and self-service throughout the journey. [northbridg...surance.ca], [broker.nor...surance.ca]
Role: UX Designer & Front-End Developer

Tools: Figma, React, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

## Problem

The current experience follows a simple pattern:

Incident Occurs → Submit Claim → Wait → Receive Updates

This creates several challenges:
- Limited visibility into claim progress
- Unclear next steps after submission
- No centralized document management
- Increased customer uncertainty and support inquiries

## Goal

Design a claims experience that helps customers:
-	Understand what happens next
-	Track claim progress
-	Upload and manage documents easily
-	Complete claims with greater confidence

## Solution

### Guided Claim Submission

Break the process into simple, manageable steps:
- What happened?
- Incident details
- Upload documents
- Review & submit

Outcome: Reduced complexity and improved usability.

### Claims Status Tracker

Provide real-time visibility into claim progress.

✓ Submitted
✓ Documents Received
⏳ Assessment In Progress
○ Settlement Review
○ Closed

Outcome: Increased transparency and reduced uncertainty.

### Document Upload Hub

Allow users to upload and track required files from a single location.
Outcome: Faster document collection and fewer missing items.

### Activity Timeline

Show a complete history of claim updates.

- Sept 12 Claim Submitted
- Sept 13 Adjuster Assigned
- Sept 15 Assessment Started

Outcome: Improved trust and self-service.

### Front-End Build
Built responsive React components including:
- Multi-step claim form
- Progress tracker
- Activity timeline
- Document upload interface
- Mobile-friendly dashboard

Using:
- React
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

### Expected Impact
Customer Benefits
- Less uncertainty
- Better understanding of claim status
- Improved user experience

### Business Benefits
- Fewer support inquiries
- Higher digital adoption
- Greater customer satisfaction

## Key Takeaway
This project transforms the claims experience from "submit and wait" into "track and understand," giving customers greater visibility and control during a critical moment in their insurance journey.
